# The pill, 2026-11-02

The white pill of PR #66 was rejected ("kind of blocky and bigger and not as slim and minimalist as
before"). The black pill (commit `c69da1b52a`) is the base again, made slimmer. Measured from the
rendered page (`#p` bounding box, 1x), same states in each version. Wispr Flow's own pill artwork is
97 x 28 px (`wispr-audit-2026-11-01/frames/flowbar-pill.svg`), with a cancel and a confirm button.

New rules: black in every theme, no light variant; 24 px tall; a waveform only while listening,
speaking or dictating; otherwise one short word; soft text only for an error or where words go
("to Sessions"); no icon, no button, no shadow beyond a 1 px edge; the bars move when a level
arrives, not on a timer; the tray's capability mark stays on the tray icon only.
