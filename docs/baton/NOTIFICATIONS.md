# Notifications policy

Decided 2026-11-02 (D152). Tessaya never sends a push notification to a phone or any other device, and never contacts a service to deliver one. Everything it says to him arrives in one of three places, all on the computer he is using:

1. **In the window.** The Now page (what needs him, what it is keeping track of, what it held back and why), Approvals, Jobs, and the cards it shows.
2. **A card.** An offer (Yes, Later, Never), a notice that something needs him, the morning brief, the line that a vocabulary word was learned. Cards expire into "Later" rather than staying forever.
3. **Spoken.** Only when he has chosen that for that kind of notice (Settings, Presence and Assistant: Off, a card, or a card and one spoken line). Quiet hours silence cards and speech alike.

Rules that hold for every kind:

- Each kind has its own switch and a budget (offers: at most 3 an hour by default). The same event is not announced twice.
- "Stop telling me about X" and "Never" silence a topic until he takes it back on the Now page.
- Nothing is announced about work that has not been looked at on his own machine: Tessaya reads state, it does not message anyone.
- A notice is never the only record. What it said is on the Now page and in History.
- Sending a message to a person or a channel is a different thing from a notification: it asks aloud for that act, every time, and money is refused.

What this means for a phone: no alert reaches it from Tessaya. T3's own mobile app may have behaviour of its own that Tessaya does not control and has not tested.
