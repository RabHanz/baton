# Baton mark concepts, round two (2026-11-01)

Brief from the Director after round one: only Wave B, Downbeat and Echo B were somewhat good; drop the rest. "Don't take any literal meaning from the word Baton. It needs to embody the vision and the capabilities that it supports, not exactly its name." So no conductor, no relay, no letter-driven concept as the source; the B stays optional, and his three picks stay in. The prompts never use the word baton (see `prompts/`).

Drawn from `docs/baton/VISION.md`: always listening, voice, one assistant across every device and session, hands-free presence, understanding, a capable companion at work.

## What was made

35 raw images through the Codex image tool (`codex-imagegen.sh`, gpt-image-2): five takes on each of seven directions (listening rings and dots; waveform B; echo B; many sessions into one; voice and sound; presence and understanding; companion at work). `raw-35-part1.png` and `raw-35-part2.png` show all 35 numbered. Curated to a dozen on `concept-sheet.png`: his three picks (hand-drawn or redrawn from round one) plus nine generated takes, each cut, traced and redrawn by hand as a few primitives (`svg/`, audit scores 99 to 100). Rejected on sight: a knotted ring that reads as an existing AI company's mark, an infinity loop, a sparkle in a disc, a busy hub, the generic wifi and speech-bubble forms.

Quota: 41 images in the two rounds (six in round one, 35 here), about 22 seconds each, on the ChatGPT subscription through Codex, no per-image charge, against plan limits. The remaining balance cannot be read from here. Raw PNGs carry a content-credentials marker and are not committed.
