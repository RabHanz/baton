Every frame here was opened and looked at. `baton-*` frames are the shipped pages rendered in Chromium
at 175 %, on light, dark and busy colour backgrounds, with no window on anyone's desktop. The same
pages were also seen on his real display (lab run `after1`, frames kept in
`D:\baton-voice-lab\lab\after1` because they show his own work).
