# Baton mark concepts, round three (2026-11-01)

The Director asked for more concepts. These are new directions, not more versions of round two (`logo-concepts-round2-2026-11-01`: Listening ripples, Wave B, Echo B, Downbeat and friends). Same rule: nothing literal from the word Baton; draw from the vision (always listening, voice, one assistant across every device and session, understanding, a capable companion). Prompts in `prompts/` never use the word baton.

31 raw images through the Codex image tool (`codex-imagegen.sh`, gpt-image-2), one at a time to spare the box's memory, about 22 s each, on the ChatGPT subscription (no per-image charge, against plan limits; 72 images across the three rounds). `raw-31-part1.png` and `raw-31-part2.png` show them all, numbered. Ten were redrawn by hand as a few primitives (`svg/`).

Dropped after redrawing, on my own review: a pinwheel that came out as a crosshair, a horizon that read as a cap, an arch-and-dot that is just headphones, a slider that is a UI toggle, calm waves that look like a menu icon, a ring-and-bar that reads as a power button. Dropped from the raw set on sight: a camera shutter, a keyhole, a wallet, a check in a circle, play buttons, a hex nut.

| Concept | Idea | How it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open crescent (recommended) | A listener holding a dot | Strong at every size; moon-like |
| Tangent ripples | Rings spreading from one tap | Good large; mush at 16 px |
| Sound arches | Sound as stacked arches | Bold; could read as a rainbow |
| Held memory | A bracket holding a dot | Calm; reads like a letter C or E |
| Spiral ear | A spiral that listens | Evocative; a snail at small sizes (weak) |
| Pill | Our own pill as the mark | Thin at small sizes (weak) |
| Basket weave | One fabric across every device | Scatters into dots at small sizes (weak) |
| Pinwheel bars | Four parts around a gap | Bold; reads at small sizes |
| Two halves | A conversation, two halves and a dot | Reads as an H or a pause sign |
| Settling disc | Knowledge settling | Bold; sunset-like |

Recommendation: Open crescent for round three; across all rounds, Listening ripples (round two) and Open crescent are the two that say "listening" without being a letter. No icon change and no kit until he picks.
