# Baton mark concepts, 2026-11-01

Eight hand-drawn SVG concepts (`svg/`), one contact sheet (`contact-sheet.png`): each mark light on black and dark on white at 16, 32, 64 and 256 px. Monochrome on purpose: a mark that only works in colour does not hold at tray size. No image generation was used. The shipped icon is **not** replaced; the Director picks first. The capability states (listening, muted, and so on) stay in the tray icon, separate from the brand mark.

Direction taken from the taste library (Quiet Geometric: one hard shape, monochrome, no gradient, silence around it). The current mark, three vertical bars in a rounded tile, is what Wispr Flow's own icon is too, which is why it reads as generic.

| # | Concept | What it says | How it holds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1a | Wave B (his idea) | A B built from horizontal bars: two stacked bowls on a flat edge, the lower one wider, the way a level meter lies on its side | Clear B at 64 and 256 px. The thin bars blur at 16 px, so the tray would need a heavier cut with fewer bars |
| 2 | Baton and beat | A conductor's baton with the beat dot at its tip | Holds at every size; reads as a pen or a comet before a baton |
| 3 | One stroke B, tip dot | One continuous line drawn as a B, starting at the dot | A B at every size; the dot is a little heavy against the spine at 256 px |
| 4 | Downbeat | An open ring with the beat dot in the gap | The cleanest at 16 px; a spinner to some eyes |
| 5 | Handoff | Two points and a line passing between them | Holds; reads as "connected" rather than as a name |
| 6 | Solid B | Bold geometric B in two stacked halves with a hairline gap | The strongest at small sizes and the plainest; Vercel-like |
| 7 | Relay rings | Two overlapping rings, one thing handed from one login to another | Holds; looks like a payment card mark |
| 8 | Echo B | A B whose bowls are nested outlines, a sound spreading | A B at 64 px and up; the nesting clogs at 16 px |

First-pass notes, for the record: the first bar-stack version read as a bar chart and the first baton read as a prohibition sign, so both were redrawn. 1a is the second attempt at his idea.
