# Screen companion

Ask about what is on your screen, be taught an app step by step, or have Tessaya do a task.

## Start

Press **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+B**, type, press Enter (Esc closes it), or say a wake word, or use **Ask Tessaya** in the tray. What you say picks the mode:

- **Look**: "what's this error?", "where is the export button?". It looks at your screen, answers aloud and on a card, and a pointer flies to what it means.
- **Teach**: "teach me how to ...", "walk me through ...". One step at a time; do the step and it notices and gives the next.
- **Drive**: "do it for me", "take over". It shows a numbered plan of where it will click. **Nothing happens until you press Run it.**

## Good to know

- It sees the screen only when you ask, and asks for consent the first time (Screen page). "Always allow screen capture" is off by default.
- Say "stop" or use **Clear the screen** in the tray to take everything down.
- The pointer and card stay until you have had time to read them.
