# Dictation

Turn speech into text in any app.

## Start

Hold **Ctrl+Alt+Space**, speak, release. The text is typed where your cursor is. **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Space** toggles it on and off instead of holding. The keys appear after the speech model has downloaded (Voice page, or tick Always listening). **Double-tap** the hold key (two quick taps) and Tessaya keeps listening after you let go; press the key once more to finish. Turn it off on the Dictation page (Double-tap to keep talking). Say "dictate" to dictate by voice and "stop dictating" to end it.

## Good to know

- With **Listen for my wake word** off, the dictation key is ready about 15 seconds after Tessaya starts, so it does not slow the start; with it on, at once.
- **Tidy what I said** removes filler words and fixes punctuation. Turn it off on the Dictation page if you want your words exactly.
- **Writing style per app** lets chat apps stay casual and documents stay formal.
- **Recognition** is done on this computer by default, and nothing you say leaves it. To use a cloud model instead, choose **Cloud** under Dictation, Recognition, pick OpenAI, Google Gemini or OpenRouter, and paste your own API key. Then your dictation and what you say to Tessaya are sent to that provider. The key is encrypted by your computer's account and never written to settings or logs. **Cloud model** lists the provider's models that can transcribe, with a recommended one, and **Test** sends a short sample clip (not your voice) and shows how long it took. If the cloud does not answer within a few seconds, this computer hears that sentence instead, so nothing you said is lost. Your languages are passed on as a hint when you speak one. Wake word checks always run on this computer, so audio from a false wake is never sent anywhere.
- It learns words from your edits; they are listed on the Dictation page.
- The last transcript is kept there in case a paste went to the wrong place. Dictated text never goes to the assistant as a command.
- On the phone there is no dictation into other apps (phones do not allow it); use the phone keyboard's own dictation.
