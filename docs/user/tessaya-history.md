# History

Everything you said and what it became, kept on this computer until you delete it.

## Start

Open the **History** page. Search by words; each row has your raw words and the cleaned text, with buttons to copy, retry, or delete.

## Good to know

- Secrets you speak are removed from what is kept.
- Nothing here is sent anywhere. Deleting a row deletes it.
- To search past agent work across projects, ask the assistant ("what did we decide about pricing?"); that reads a separate history index on this computer, or on a server you paired for memory sync that has one. If neither can be reached, the assistant says so.

## Your voice report

The Insights page has a **Your voice** section made from History alone: how many words you dictate at a time, your filler words, how often Tessaya changes what it heard, and the words you keep correcting (fix the same word twice and it appears, so you can add it to your dictionary). Nothing leaves the computer.
